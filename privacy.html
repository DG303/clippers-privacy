Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Clippers Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to YouTube channels the owner operates.

What it accesses

It requests a single Google permission, youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data.

What it stores

An OAuth token for each channel, kept only on the owner's own computers. It is never shared with third parties. Uploaded videos and their titles and descriptions are published to YouTube under YouTube's own terms.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
